Torrance Service Area

Torrance Express Plumbing is based in Torrance, CA and dispatches across the South Bay around the clock. We cover Torrance's standard ZIP codes, 90501 through 90505, plus ZIP code 90277 for the Hollywood Riviera neighborhood, and we serve ten surrounding cities and communities, grouped below by the direction they lie from Torrance.

Torrance neighborhoods

Torrance is a large city of roughly 140,000 people, and its neighborhoods have different housing ages and different plumbing needs. These are the areas we dispatch to every day.

Old Torrance

The original 1912 town, bounded roughly by Madrona Avenue, 190th Street, Western Avenue, and Sepulveda Boulevard. Its downtown was laid out on a diagonal, and many of the houses on its first residential streets, Gramercy and Andreo avenues, have passed their hundredth birthday.

Central Torrance and Del Amo

The commercial center of the city, anchored by the Del Amo Fashion Center and the Torrance Promenade, with offices and retail along 190th Street between Hawthorne Boulevard and Western Avenue. Madrona Marsh Preserve lies just east of the mall.

West Torrance and Southwood

West Torrance lies west of Hawthorne Boulevard between 190th Street and Torrance Boulevard. Southwood continues south from Torrance Boulevard to Sepulveda Boulevard. Both are largely mid-century tract neighborhoods.

Hollywood Riviera

On the northern edge of the Palos Verdes Peninsula, south of Pacific Coast Highway and west of Hawthorne Boulevard. Addresses here use ZIP code 90277, which is a Redondo Beach postal address, although the neighborhood is part of Torrance.

Walteria

A neighborhood in south Torrance, annexed to the city in 1928, with its own post office on Pacific Coast Highway and a branch of the Torrance library.

North Torrance

The area around Crenshaw Boulevard on the city's northern side, with its own post office and library branch, close to the refinery at the north end of the city and to the borders with Lawndale and Gardena.

Southeast Torrance

The part of the city around Zamperini Field, Torrance's general aviation airport, toward the Harbor Gateway and Harbor City borders.

Cities and communities we serve

Torrance borders Redondo Beach, Lawndale, Gardena, Lomita, Rolling Hills Estates, Palos Verdes Estates, the unincorporated community of Alondra Park, and the Los Angeles neighborhoods of Harbor City and Harbor Gateway. We grouped our service area the way our crews actually drive it.

Also covered
