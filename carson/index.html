Commercial plumbing, at a real scale

Carson generates more commercial plumbing calls, relative to its size, than most of the cities we serve around Torrance. That's a direct result of the city's own history: incorporated later than its neighbors, in 1968, Carson absorbed a heavier concentration of industrial and commercial land use, refineries, waste and materials sites among them, that surrounding cities pushed out to its borders. Many of those sites have since been redeveloped into retail, offices, and event venues like Dignity Health Sports Park on the Cal State Dominguez Hills campus, but the underlying commercial and light-industrial footprint remains larger than a purely residential suburb would carry.

Before we arrive

If you have a burst pipe or active leak in Carson

Whether it is a residential leak or a commercial line failure, Carson's mixed property types mean an emergency looks different from one address to the next. Here is what to do first either way.

Shut off the main valve

Stop the water at the source first, if you can safely reach the main shutoff valve.

Kill power to wet areas

If water has reached an outlet or panel, cut power to that area rather than working around it.

Clear the immediate area

Move people, pets, and valuables away from the water while help is on the way.

Call our 24/7 line

A live dispatcher answers any hour and can walk you through the next step while a technician heads your way.
