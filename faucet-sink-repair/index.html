Signs

Common faucet and sink problems

Persistent dripping

A faucet that drips even when fully shut off usually has a worn cartridge, washer, or O-ring that needs replacement.

Leak under the sink

Water pooling in the cabinet below a sink can come from a failing supply line, a loose fitting, or a cracked drain trap.

Low flow from the faucet

Reduced flow, especially if it's gotten worse gradually, is often caused by mineral scale clogging the faucet's aerator or internal passages.

Handle loose or hard to turn

A stiff or loose handle usually means the internal cartridge or valve mechanism is wearing out and will fail completely before long.

Local factors

Why faucets scale up faster here

Los Angeles County's naturally hard water leaves mineral deposits inside faucet aerators and cartridges faster than it would in a soft-water region, which is a common reason a Torrance faucet's flow gradually weakens even without any external damage or age-related wear.

Homes closer to the coast, including parts of Walteria and Hollywood Riviera, also see faster corrosion on lower-grade fixture connections and shutoff valves from marine-layer humidity, which is part of why we check the shutoff valve's condition, not just the faucet, during a repair call.

How we diagnose it

How we handle a faucet or sink repair

Leak source isolation

We determine whether the leak is at the faucet itself, a supply line connection, or the drain assembly beneath the sink.

Component repair or replacement

Worn cartridges, O-rings, and washers are replaced as needed rather than replacing the whole faucet unnecessarily, since most faucet drips come down to one small, inexpensive part.

Supply line and valve check

We inspect the shutoff valve and supply lines while under the sink, since a corroded valve is often close to failing on its own.

Full replacement when appropriate

If a faucet is old enough or damaged enough that parts are no longer practical to source, we'll recommend replacement and help you choose a suitable fixture.

Sink repair

Beyond the faucet: sink and drain issues

Not every under-sink call is about the faucet. A cracked P-trap, a loose slip-joint connection, or a garbage disposal leaking at its mounting flange are all common causes of water appearing in a sink cabinet that have nothing to do with the faucet itself, and we check all of them during a leak call rather than assuming the faucet is the source.

For a kitchen remodel or sink replacement, we can also handle disconnecting and reconnecting plumbing for a new sink basin, including adjusting drain height and supply line length to fit the new fixture properly.

A bathroom sink pop-up drain that won't hold water, or a stopper mechanism that's stuck, is another common call that's separate from both the faucet and the P-trap. We check the full stopper linkage rather than assuming a worn cartridge is always to blame for a sink that won't drain or hold water correctly.

FAQ

Questions about faucet and sink repair in Torrance

Can you match my existing faucet brand for parts?

In most cases, yes. If an exact replacement part isn't available for an older or discontinued model, we'll walk through replacement options.

Is a dripping faucet worth fixing or just a minor annoyance?

It's worth fixing. A slow, persistent drip can waste a meaningful amount of water over a year and often signals a component that's fully failing soon.

Do you install customer-supplied faucets and sinks?

Yes, we can install a fixture you've already purchased in addition to sourcing one for you.

My bathroom sink won't hold water in the stopper. Is that a faucet issue?

No, that's usually the pop-up stopper linkage, a separate mechanism from the faucet itself, and it's a straightforward adjustment or repair that doesn't require replacing the faucet.
