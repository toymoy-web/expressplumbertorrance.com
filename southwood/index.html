About Southwood

A 1960s tract neighborhood in south Torrance

Southwood sits in south Torrance, in ZIP code 90505. Our service area page describes it as continuing south from Torrance Boulevard toward Sepulveda Boulevard.

The City of Torrance's history says Wilson Development opened the Southwood Riviera Royale tract in 1962, near Anza Avenue and Calle Mayor. The same history says Wilson built most of the houses constructed in Torrance in the 1950s and 1960s, in tracts of 200 to 500 homes, with developers required to provide streets, street lights and other infrastructure.

Local listing guides describe many single-story mid-century houses beside larger remodeled homes. Those guides are marketing summaries, so read that as a general picture and not a survey.

Plumbing here

What a house of this era calls for

A house from the early 1960s has had about six decades to be remodeled. Many owners have replaced a water heater, a set of fixtures or a section of pipe. What matters is what your house has now, so we look before we recommend.

Additions and bath remodels

A new bathroom adds demand on the supply lines and the water heater. We check that both can carry it before the work starts.

Slab or raised floor

Knowing which your house has changes how a hidden leak is found. We confirm it on site and do not guess from the neighborhood.

Water heater age

The label shows the manufacture date. We read it and check around the base for rust or moisture.

Shutoff valves

Find your main shutoff and turn it once a year. Valves that sit untouched for decades can stick.

Local FAQ

Questions from Southwood residents

Where is Southwood in Torrance?

Southwood is in south Torrance, in ZIP code 90505. Our service area page describes it as continuing south from Torrance Boulevard toward Sepulveda Boulevard.

Do all Southwood houses have the same plumbing?

No. Houses in one tract were built to similar plans, but owners have remodeled since. We check each house on its own.

How do I know if my water heater is due for replacement?

Read the label for the manufacture date and look around the base for rust or moisture. Many tank heaters last 10 to 15 years, and an older unit that leaks is usually a replacement and not a repair.

Do you handle plumbing for bathroom and kitchen remodels?

Yes. That includes fixtures, line relocations and additions. Ask about permits when you call.
