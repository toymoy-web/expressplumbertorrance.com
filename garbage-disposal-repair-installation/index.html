Signs

Common garbage disposal problems

Humming but not spinning

A disposal that hums without turning usually has a jam that can often be cleared without replacing the unit.

Leaking from the bottom

Water leaking from underneath a disposal typically means an internal seal has failed, which generally means replacement rather than repair.

No response at all

A disposal with no sound or movement may have tripped its internal reset button, a wiring issue, or a fully failed motor.

Unusual grinding noise

A grinding or rattling noise, especially with something that shouldn't be inside the unit, often means a foreign object needs to be safely removed.

Persistent odor from the unit

A lingering smell even after running the disposal usually means food debris is caught somewhere the water flow doesn't fully clear, and a deeper cleaning or inspection is worth doing.

Local factors

What we see most in Torrance kitchens

Older disposals installed decades ago in Old Torrance and Walteria kitchens are frequently well past their typical service life, and internal seal failures become more common as units age, especially units that have run continuously for a decade or more.

We also see garbage disposal leaks that are actually caused by a nearby dishwasher connection or the sink's drain assembly rather than the disposal itself, which is why we check the full connection point during a leak call rather than assuming the disposal is automatically at fault.

Kitchens in Torrance range from the original layouts of tract homes in West Torrance to open-plan remodels in Hollywood Riviera, and a disposal has to fit under the sink as it exists, not as a catalog shows it. We check cabinet depth, the dishwasher drain connection, and the outlet before choosing a unit.

How we diagnose it

How we handle a disposal call

Jam clearing

For a humming, non-spinning unit, we safely clear whatever's jamming the flywheel before assuming the motor has failed.

Leak source isolation

We confirm whether a leak is coming from the disposal's seals, the dishwasher connection, or the sink flange above it.

Reset and electrical check

For a fully unresponsive unit, we check the reset button and electrical connection before concluding the motor itself has failed.

Replacement when repair isn't practical

If the unit's motor or seals have failed, replacement is usually more cost-effective than repair, and we'll say so directly.

New installation

Installing a new or upgraded disposal

Installing a new garbage disposal, whether replacing a failed unit or upgrading to a higher-horsepower model, involves confirming the mounting assembly matches, the electrical connection is appropriate for the new unit's draw, and the dishwasher drain connection, if present, is properly routed. We handle all of this as part of installation rather than just swapping the unit itself.

For households considering an upgrade, a higher horsepower unit generally runs quieter and handles tougher food waste with less jamming, worth considering if your current unit struggles regularly.

It's also worth checking your kitchen's electrical setup during a disposal upgrade. Some older Torrance kitchens were wired for a smaller, lower-draw unit, and a higher-horsepower replacement can require confirming the circuit and switch can handle the new unit's load safely.

By symptom

Disposal problems and what usually causes them

Hums but does not spin

A jammed flywheel is the usual cause. Clearing it is often quick, unless the motor has burned out.

Leaks from the bottom or side

Water at the housing usually means a failed seal or a cracked body, and that points to replacement.

Leaks around the sink flange

A worn gasket or loose mount lets water seep under the sink. It can be resealed or the unit reset.

Backs up into the sink

A clogged drain beyond the trap can send water back up. The disposal may not be at fault.

Replace and reconnect

Installing a unit you already bought

Many callers buy a disposal first and then need help with the mounting, the dishwasher connection, and the drain. We can install a unit you supply. Tell the dispatcher the make and whether the kitchen already has a power outlet and switch.

If the sink drains slowly even without the disposal running, see our drain cleaning page. A dishwasher that leaks water from the air gap usually has a clogged drain line behind it, and we check that during the visit.

FAQ

Questions about garbage disposal service in Torrance

Can a jammed disposal usually be fixed without replacing it?

Yes, in most cases a jam can be cleared and the unit returns to normal operation without needing replacement.

How long do garbage disposals typically last?

Most residential units last 8 to 12 years with normal use, though heavier use or persistent food buildup can shorten that.

Can you install a customer-supplied disposal unit?

Yes, we can install a disposal you've already purchased in addition to sourcing one for you.

Does a higher horsepower disposal need special wiring?

Sometimes. We check the existing circuit during installation and can address wiring needs if the new unit draws more power than what's already in place.

My disposal smells bad even after running it. What's causing that?

Trapped food debris in a spot the water flow doesn't reach well is the usual cause. A thorough cleaning or, for an older unit, replacement usually resolves it.

My disposal hums but will not spin. What now?

A jammed flywheel is the common cause. A technician can free it and test the motor, and if the motor has failed, replacement is the better choice.

Is a disposal leaking from the bottom repairable?

Usually not. A leak from the housing means the seal or case has failed, so replacement is the lasting fix. A leak at the sink flange is often repairable.

Can you install a garbage disposal I already bought?

Yes. Share the brand and model when you call, and mention whether a switch and outlet are already in place.

Why does my dishwasher leak from the air gap?

The drain line behind the dishwasher is often clogged, which pushes water up through the air gap. Clearing the line normally stops it.
