About Torrance Express Plumbing

Torrance Express Plumbing provides licensed plumbing repair, installation, and 24/7 emergency service across Torrance, CA and the surrounding South Bay. We work on everything from a single dripping faucet to a full home repipe, and we size every recommendation to what your specific home and this market's water actually need, not a generic template answer.

How we work

Every job gets a clear diagnosis and a price you approve before any work begins. We don't leave a broken form on a page or bury a call behind a contact form, if you need us, you call and talk to a person.

Built for this market

Torrance's mix of housing eras, older Old Torrance bungalows, mid-century West Torrance tract homes, and newer North Torrance construction, means different homes fail in different ways. We factor in this area's naturally hard water and coastal-adjacent corrosion in how we diagnose and recommend repairs, rather than treating every call the same regardless of where the home sits or when it was built.

Licensing

Plumbing work in California requires a valid C-36 contractor license. We're happy to provide license verification details on request when you call.
