The ground here moves

Why Rolling Hills Estates plumbing is different from Torrance

Rolling Hills Estates sits on the northern side of the Palos Verdes Peninsula, facing Torrance across the hills that form Torrance's southern edge. It is a mostly residential city of about 8,300 people on 3.6 square miles, incorporated in 1957, with miles of horse trails and a housing stock that is roughly 90 percent owner-occupied. It is also built on ground with a documented history of landslides.

On July 8, 2023, the Peartree Lane landslide destroyed homes along the edge of a canyon here, and the city reported a sewer line break caused by land movement during the same period. A UCLA analysis concluded the movement likely began the previous winter. The slide belongs to the wider Palos Verdes Peninsula picture, since the Greater Portuguese Bend landslide complex also affects Rancho Palos Verdes and Rolling Hills.

What that means for a plumber

Sewer and leak work on hillside and canyon-edge lots

Camera inspection after any ground shift

If a lot has settled, cracked, or shifted, a sewer camera can show a displaced joint or a break long before a backup does.

Leaks and saturated soil

Water leaking into a slope adds to the soil's water content. Finding and fixing a hidden leak is a sensible step, but it does not replace a geotechnical evaluation of the lot.

Steep and canyon-edge runs

Sewer and drain lines on steep lots sit under more stress than flat-lot lines, and repairs often benefit from trenchless methods that disturb less of the slope.

Emergency dispatch around the clock

A burst line or sewer break does not wait for morning, and our emergency line covers Rolling Hills Estates any hour of the day or night.

Noticed cracking, settling, or a wet spot on your lot?

A sewer camera and leak trace can rule a failing line in or out.

What to expect from a Rolling Hills Estates service call

Rolling Hills Estates is a short drive over the hill from our Torrance base, so response times here run close to our core coverage. The Promenade on the Peninsula and the Peninsula Center shopping area are the easiest reference points when a customer is describing where a property is relative to the main roads.

Coverage reaches the surrounding ZIP codes as well, including Torrance (90505, 90503), Lomita (90717), and Rancho Palos Verdes (90275), for sewer line repair, leak detection, and water line work.

Water here comes from California Water Service, which has served the Palos Verdes Peninsula since 1970, a different utility than the municipal system that serves most of Torrance. Hardness is similar, and the water is imported through the same regional wholesale network.

A plumber is not a geotechnical engineer, and we do not give opinions on whether a slope is stable. What we can do is document the condition of your water and sewer lines so that you, your insurer, and the city's geologist have accurate information.

Before we arrive

If you have a burst pipe or active leak in Rolling Hills Estates

On hillside lots, escaping water has somewhere to go that flat lots do not. Here is what to do first if you have an active leak or burst pipe.

Shut off the main valve

Stop the water at the source, if you can safely reach the main shutoff.

Kill power to wet areas

If water has reached an outlet or panel, cut power to that area.

Clear the immediate area

Move people, pets, and valuables away from the water.

Call our 24/7 line

A live dispatcher answers any hour and can talk you through the next step.

Local FAQ

Questions from Rolling Hills Estates residents

Can a plumber tell if a leak is contributing to ground movement?

We can confirm whether a water or sewer line on your property is leaking or broken and repair it. Whether the ground is moving, and why, is a question for a geotechnical engineer or your city's geologist, and we are glad to coordinate with them.

Do you do sewer camera inspections on hillside lots?

Yes. A camera inspection shows displaced joints, cracks, and low spots in a line, and it is the most useful first step on any lot that has settled or shifted.

Who supplies water in Rolling Hills Estates?

California Water Service serves Rolling Hills Estates through its Palos Verdes district, which has operated on the Peninsula since 1970. Most of Torrance is on a separate municipal system.

Do you cover every ZIP code in Rolling Hills Estates?

Yes, we cover both Rolling Hills Estates ZIP codes, 90274 and 90275, along with the neighboring Torrance, Lomita, and Rancho Palos Verdes ZIP codes.

Is emergency service available in Rolling Hills Estates?

Yes, our 24/7 emergency line covers Rolling Hills Estates the same as the rest of our service area, including sewer breaks and burst pipes after hours.
