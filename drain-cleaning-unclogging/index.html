Signs

Signs your drain needs professional cleaning

Slow-draining sinks or tubs

Water backing up or draining slowly in more than one fixture usually points to a clog further down the line, not just a single trap.

Gurgling sounds

Air trapped behind a partial clog makes a gurgling noise in drains or toilets when another fixture runs.

Recurring clogs

A drain that clogs again within weeks of snaking usually has root intrusion or a structural issue, not just debris.

Sewage odor indoors

A persistent sewage smell, especially near a floor drain, can mean a blocked or cracked sewer line rather than a simple clog.

Local factors

Why Torrance drains clog the way they do

Torrance's older neighborhoods, including Old Torrance and parts of Walteria, still run on clay or cast-iron sewer laterals from the city's early- and mid-20th-century growth. Those materials are more prone to root intrusion from mature street trees and to gradual cracking than the PVC lines used in newer construction.

Los Angeles County's naturally hard water also plays a role: mineral scale builds up inside pipe walls over time, narrowing the effective diameter of a line and giving grease and debris less room to pass through before a clog forms.

In Old Torrance, the Olmsted-designed original tract dates to 1912, and Gramercy and Andreo avenues, the city's first two residential streets, have houses that passed the century mark in 2012. A recurring clog in a home of that age is worth a camera inspection of the lateral rather than another round of snaking.

How we diagnose it

How we find the real cause

Initial assessment

Mechanical clearing

A drain snake or auger clears standard clogs from sinks, tubs, and toilets in most calls.

Camera inspection

For recurring or main-line clogs, a sewer camera confirms whether the cause is roots, grease, a belly in the line, or a cracked pipe.

High-pressure water jetting clears root intrusion and grease buildup that a snake alone can't fully remove.

The choice

Snaking vs. hydro jetting

Standard snaking

  • Lower cost for a simple clog
  • Fast for isolated blockages
  • Doesn't clear grease coating pipe walls
  • Root intrusion often returns quickly

Hydro jetting

  • Clears the full pipe diameter
  • Removes grease and root fragments
  • Better for recurring clogs
  • Camera-confirmed before recommending
Prevention

Keeping a Torrance drain from clogging again

A few habits make a real difference between service calls: scrape grease into the trash instead of rinsing it down a kitchen drain, since grease re-solidifies inside pipes as it cools and is one of the most common causes of a repeat clog. A simple mesh screen over bathroom and shower drains catches hair before it forms a mat further down the line.

For a Torrance property with mature street trees nearby, a recurring main-line clog is often a root-intrusion issue rather than something a screen can prevent. In those cases, a periodic hydro jetting service, done on a schedule rather than only after a backup, keeps roots from re-establishing inside the line between visits.

If your home is on the older side, worth asking us about during a service call is what your sewer lateral is actually made of. A cast-iron or clay lateral nearing the end of its service life will keep generating clogs no matter how carefully you maintain the fixtures upstream of it, and knowing that up front changes whether ongoing cleaning or a lateral repair is the better long-term answer.

Read the symptoms

Which drain is backing up tells us where the clog sits

Where the water misbehaves is the best clue to how deep the blockage is. A single slow sink usually means a local clog in its own trap or branch. When several fixtures act up together, the problem is further down the line.

One fixture only

A slow sink, tub, or shower normally has hair, soap, or grease close to the drain. This is the quickest kind of clog to clear.

Lowest drain fills first

Water rising in a basement or ground-level drain while upstairs fixtures run points to a blockage in the main line.

Gurgling toilet

Bubbling in the bowl after another fixture drains means air is being pushed past a partial blockage.

Kitchen sink into the laundry sink

When one sink empties into another, the shared branch is blocked. A garbage disposal can add to the problem.

Choosing the method

Snake, cable machine, or camera first

A hand snake clears a close clog from a sink or tub. A cable machine reaches further and cuts through roots and dense buildup. When a clog keeps coming back, a camera shows what is really sitting in the pipe so we do not guess.

If grease or roots keep returning, hydro jetting washes the pipe walls clean instead of poking a hole through the blockage. If the camera finds a cracked or sagging section, the fix moves to sewer line repair. While you wait for us, stop running water into the affected drains and keep chemical cleaners out of the pipe, since they can burn a technician's skin and damage old fittings.

Questions about drain cleaning in Torrance

Can you clear a main sewer line, not just a sink?

Yes. We handle main line clogs with camera inspection and hydro jetting when a standard snake isn't enough.

Do you offer drain cleaning after hours?

Yes, our emergency line covers drain cleaning 24 hours a day, including nights, weekends, and holidays.

Will you show me what caused the clog?

When we run a camera inspection, we can show you the footage so you understand what caused the blockage.

Can you work directly with a property manager or landlord?

Yes, we regularly coordinate directly with property managers and landlords on behalf of tenants, including scheduling and providing documentation of the work performed.

Why is my kitchen sink backing up into the laundry sink?

Both fixtures usually share one drain branch. When that branch clogs, water takes the easiest path out through the other opening. Clearing the branch fixes both, and a camera can confirm nothing deeper is wrong.

I already ran a snake and nothing changed. What now?

A short household snake often stops at the first bend or only pokes a hole in the clog. A longer cable machine or a camera look can reach what it missed.

Can you tell a simple clog from a sewer line problem?

Usually, yes. One slow fixture points to a local clog. Several fixtures backing up together, or a drain that clogs every few months, points to the main line and deserves a camera inspection.

How soon can you come for a backed-up drain?

Backups are handled through our 24/7 emergency line, and same-day visits are the goal for routine clogs. Call and the dispatcher will tell you what is open today.

Will you clear a drain that backs up during showers?

Yes. Water that comes up in the tub when another fixture runs usually means the shared branch or main line is partly blocked, and that is the kind of call we see often.
