Signs

Signs your drain needs professional cleaning

Slow-draining sinks or tubs

Water backing up or draining slowly in more than one fixture usually points to a clog further down the line, not just a single trap.

Gurgling sounds

Air trapped behind a partial clog makes a gurgling noise in drains or toilets when another fixture runs.

Recurring clogs

A drain that clogs again within weeks of snaking usually has root intrusion or a structural issue, not just debris.

Sewage odor indoors

A persistent sewage smell, especially near a floor drain, can mean a blocked or cracked sewer line rather than a simple clog.

Local factors

Why Torrance drains clog the way they do

Torrance's older neighborhoods, including Old Torrance and parts of Walteria, still run on clay or cast-iron sewer laterals from the city's early- and mid-20th-century growth. Those materials are more prone to root intrusion from mature street trees and to gradual cracking than the PVC lines used in newer construction.

Los Angeles County's naturally hard water also plays a role: mineral scale builds up inside pipe walls over time, narrowing the effective diameter of a line and giving grease and debris less room to pass through before a clog forms.

How we diagnose it

How we find the real cause

Initial assessment

We ask about which fixtures are affected and how often the clog recurs before running any equipment.

Mechanical clearing

A drain snake or auger clears standard clogs from sinks, tubs, and toilets in most calls.

Camera inspection

For recurring or main-line clogs, a sewer camera confirms whether the cause is roots, grease, a belly in the line, or a cracked pipe.

Hydro jetting when needed

High-pressure water jetting clears root intrusion and grease buildup that a snake alone can't fully remove.

The choice

Snaking vs. hydro jetting

Standard snaking

  • Lower cost for a simple clog
  • Fast for isolated blockages
  • Doesn't clear grease coating pipe walls
  • Root intrusion often returns quickly

Hydro jetting

  • Clears the full pipe diameter
  • Removes grease and root fragments
  • Better for recurring clogs
  • Camera-confirmed before recommending
Prevention

Keeping a Torrance drain from clogging again

A few habits make a real difference between service calls: scrape grease into the trash instead of rinsing it down a kitchen drain, since grease re-solidifies inside pipes as it cools and is one of the most common causes of a repeat clog. A simple mesh screen over bathroom and shower drains catches hair before it forms a mat further down the line.

For a Torrance property with mature street trees nearby, a recurring main-line clog is often a root-intrusion issue rather than something a screen can prevent. In those cases, a periodic hydro jetting service, done on a schedule rather than only after a backup, keeps roots from re-establishing inside the line between visits.

If your home is on the older side, worth asking us about during a service call is what your sewer lateral is actually made of. A cast-iron or clay lateral nearing the end of its service life will keep generating clogs no matter how carefully you maintain the fixtures upstream of it, and knowing that up front changes whether ongoing cleaning or a lateral repair is the better long-term answer.
